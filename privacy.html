Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Catalyst Alerter is a personal software project. This policy explains the limited information associated with the project and its SMS notification workflow.

Information processed

The monitoring system processes publicly available information from regulatory, clinical-trial, SEC, company-announcement, and news sources. For SMS delivery, AWS may process the destination phone number and delivery-related metadata as necessary to provide the messaging service.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the event-monitoring and notification workflow. Catalyst Alerter does not sell personal information and does not use SMS recipient information for advertising.

Sharing

Information may be processed by infrastructure providers used to operate the project, including Amazon Web Services, solely as required to provide hosting, storage, monitoring, and notification functionality.

Retention

Operational logs and application state may be retained for troubleshooting, reliability, and event-deduplication purposes. Retention is limited to what is reasonably needed for operation of the project.

SMS data

SMS consent information and destination phone numbers are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed through the project operator's public GitHub profile: github.com/K3v1njk.
