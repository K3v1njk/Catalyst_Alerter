SMS Use & Consent

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Purpose of SMS messages

Catalyst Alerter uses SMS only to deliver time-sensitive notifications generated by the operator's biotech event-monitoring software. Messages may relate to public regulatory, clinical-trial, SEC-filing, or company-announcement events detected by the system.

Who receives messages

The service is operated for personal use. SMS messages are sent only to a phone number controlled by the account owner/project operator. Catalyst Alerter does not operate a public SMS marketing list and does not send promotional messages to third parties.

Consent and opt-in

The account owner explicitly initiates and confirms the AWS notification subscription for the phone number used by this project. No phone number is added through scraped, purchased, rented, or third-party marketing lists.

Message frequency

Message frequency is variable and event-driven. The system sends notifications only when a configured monitoring rule identifies a relevant event. During periods with no events, no SMS messages may be sent.

Opt-out

The account owner may stop SMS delivery at any time by removing or unsubscribing the phone number from the associated AWS SNS topic/subscription. Carrier or AWS-supported opt-out mechanisms may also apply depending on the origination identity used.

Costs

Standard carrier messaging and data rates may apply.

Support

For project-related questions, use the public GitHub profile: github.com/K3v1njk.

No financial advice

Alerts are informational software notifications generated from public sources. They are not investment recommendations, trading instructions, or financial advice.
